From geometry to flow field - exploring aerodynamics through CFD.

:

Recently, I designed and simulated a customized tubercles airfoil using COMSOL Multiphysics, working through the complete simulation workflow from geometry preparation to flow-field visualization.

The airfoil was developed with a 100 mm chord and 100 mm span, incorporating my own leading-edge tubercle configuration with 7 mm amplitude and 25 mm wavelength.

The study involved developing the computational fluid domain, setting up a turbulent SST k-wk-\omega model, defining boundary conditions, generating the mesh, and analyzing the resulting velocity, pressure and streamline distributions.

What I found particularly interesting was being able to visually connect the airfoil geometry with the resulting flow behavior - especially the changes in velocity and pressure around the leading edge and the development of the downstream wake.

I'm now taking the study further by investigating leading-edge tubercle modifications and their influence on aerodynamic flow characteristics.

This project has been a great hands-on learning experience in CFD modelling, fluid mechanics and aerodynamic simulation, while also highlighting how important proper geometry, boundary conditions and mesh quality are for obtaining meaningful results.

Software: COMSOL Multiphysics
